Pricing & trial

Try Otto in your practice day.

The public offer includes a trial of 10 signed notes or 30 days, whichever comes first. No card is required for the advertised trial.

Pricing detail to confirm. The public page does not clearly identify whether $99 is billed per provider or per practice. Confirm the billing unit, payment-processing fees, included usage, taxes, and cancellation terms before subscribing. This review page does not assume those terms.

What to evaluate during the trial.

Documentation

Try the available note formats with fictitious data first. Review draft quality, corrections, signature, and the proposed chart updates.

Your weekly workflow

Check recurring scheduling, telehealth, patient records, and payment setup against the work your practice needs.

Access after the trial

The public site says AI capabilities pause until subscription and records remain accessible in read-only form. Confirm export and retention details separately.
