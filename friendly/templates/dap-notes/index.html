DAP notes, with room for your judgment.

We use this format to show how the pieces of an encounter fit together. Explore a fictional example, then keep a blank template for your own approved workflow.

At a glance

DAP groups the session information under Data, followed by your assessment and plan.

What goes in each section?

D

Data

The client’s report, your observations, interventions, and response.

A

Assessment

Your interpretation of the session and progress toward goals.

P

Plan

The agreed next steps and follow-up.

A fictional DAP example

Educational excerpt written by Otto editorial. This is not a complete clinical record or an output from Otto’s AI. It omits patient identifiers, diagnoses, and risk findings; a real record must reflect the actual encounter and applicable requirements. Clinical review is pending.

Data

The fictional client reports completing a planned evening walk on three days and describes feeling more able to pause before responding to work messages. During the fictional session, the client identifies one difficult work interaction and participates in a rehearsal of a response they would like to try.

Assessment

The example shows the client practicing a previously discussed skill. A real assessment would connect the observed response to the established treatment goals and document the clinician’s actual findings.

Plan

What we check before a note is final

  • The note reflects the actual encounter and distinguishes reports, observations, and clinical interpretation.
  • Relevant interventions, the client’s response, and progress toward treatment goals are described.
  • Required encounter details, assessments, decisions, follow-up, and signature are completed through the practice’s approved workflow.
  • Copied text is reviewed for stale details or statements unsupported by this visit.
