Step 1 of 4

Visit preparation

Review the patient record and last-session context. Bring your own clinical judgment to the summary before starting the next visit.

Patient context → Visit preparation
Step 2 of 4

Draft and review the note

With the appropriate consent, capture session audio and generate a draft. Check the SOAP or DAP structure, correct the details, and sign the note when it reflects the encounter.

Session audio → Draft → Clinician review → Signature
Step 3 of 4

Confirm proposed record updates

Review extracted diagnoses, treatment-plan items, and medication changes. Accept the items that belong in the record and dismiss those that do not.

Signed note → Proposed updates → Your confirmation
Step 4 of 4

Review the charge and payment

Check the prefilled code against the service and documentation. Review the fee and payment details before using the collection workflow.

Reviewed service → Charge review → Payment
