Get the transfer scope in writing.

Ask Otto to confirm accepted file formats, importable fields, attachments, signatures, historical notes, balances, calendars, fees, timing, and the secure transfer method. Import support has not been verified in this review site.

Test a small, representative sample.

Agree on the test and acceptance criteria with the receiving team. Compare record counts and inspect document readability, author attribution, dates, and links to the correct client. Track exceptions before transferring more records.

Protect continuity of care.

Plan an overlap period, explain any portal or payment changes to clients, and preserve access to records under your practice’s retention obligations. Close an old account only after the responsible owner has accepted the transfer and confirmed continued record access.
