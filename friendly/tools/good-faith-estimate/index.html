What goes in the estimate?

For a patient who is uninsured or not using insurance, include patient name and birth date, the primary service, the estimate date, the expected services and charges, and provider contact, NPI and taxpayer ID. Include diagnosis and service codes when applicable. Itemize expected charges for the period of care; identify any separately scheduled services.

Dispute threshold. A patient may qualify for the federal patient-provider dispute process when a provider's bill is at least $400 above that provider's estimate. The deadline is generally 120 calendar days from the original bill.

Recurring services may be estimated for up to 12 months. CMS's standard form has more detail and must be checked for each practice's situation. This version is intended for one provider only. If other providers or facilities are reasonably expected to furnish care during the period, use the full CMS form and add their estimates as applicable. A generator cannot certify legal compliance.
