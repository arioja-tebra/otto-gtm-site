What the calculation includes

Monthly average sessions = clinicians × weekly sessions × working weeks ÷ 12. We apply card processing to the selected share of session payments. AI notes are based on sessions for AI-enabled clinicians, and we charge overages only above the practice-level allowance you enter.

We normalize annual subscription prices to monthly amounts, add recurring fees, and calculate the first year as 12 months of recurring cost plus setup, migration, and overlap. Other annual fees are divided by 12. We assume one card transaction per card-paid session and treat claims as a separate practice-level input.

This excludes taxes, discounts, refunds, chargebacks, financing, and the value of staff time. It does not compare feature quality, clinical suitability, or guarantee vendor charges.
