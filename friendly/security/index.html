Trust & review

Clear decisions.
Careful handling of data.

Understand the review controls Otto describes, and get specific answers about the data your practice entrusts to a system.

What Otto publicly describes.

Clinician confirmation

The public workflow requires confirmation before proposed structured data writes to the longitudinal record.

Draft attribution

Otto describes an AI provenance badge and a note-review process where the clinician can edit and sign.

Correction and recovery

The site describes correction tracking and a fallback to re-recording or manual entry if a note fails.

Questions to resolve before using patient data.

These items need written, Otto-specific answers. A general product description does not establish contractual protections or a complete security posture.

Business associate agreement and service scope

Request the applicable BAA and confirm which Otto services, AI processing, telehealth, and support workflows it covers. BAA availability has not been verified in this review.

Recording consent, retention, and deletion

Confirm how consent is captured, how long audio and transcripts persist, how deletion works, and whether backups have a different retention period. Ask what happens when a patient declines recording.

AI providers and use of data

Request the subprocessor list and contractual limits on training use, human access, retention, and regional processing. Do not infer these terms from the presence of an AI feature.

Access controls and incident response

Confirm authentication, roles, audit logging, encryption, account offboarding, and incident notification procedures. Ask which controls your practice can configure.

Record export and account closure

Request an export demonstration and written terms for format, timing, retention, and deletion after cancellation or the end of a trial.
