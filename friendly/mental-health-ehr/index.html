Come in with the context.

02 / AFTER THE CONVERSATION

Make it your note.

Let’s talk through the details

A few things you might be wondering.

We’ll help you find the details you need before choosing a new home for your practice.

Who is Otto for?

Otto is built for mental health providers. Confirm that its current practice-size, specialty, and payment workflows fit your needs during evaluation.

Does Otto write and sign the note for me?

Otto drafts a SOAP or DAP note from session audio with patient consent. You review, edit, and sign. You also confirm proposed updates before they enter the patient record.

Can I bring records from my current EHR?

Record import formats and support need to be confirmed with Otto. Our switching guides help you prepare an inventory and questions for the team.

What about insurance billing?

Confirm supported claims and payer workflows with the team. Payment collection and prefilled procedure codes do not establish insurance-claims support.

How much does Otto cost?

The public site advertises $99/month after a trial of 10 signed notes or 30 days, whichever comes first. The billing unit and additional fees need confirmation. Review pricing and trial details.

Where can I ask about privacy and recording?

Our security page brings together questions about the BAA, recording consent, retention, subprocessors, access, and exports. Request written terms for your Otto service before using patient data.
