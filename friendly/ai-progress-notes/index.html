A closer look

Your questions about AI notes.

Can I edit an AI-drafted note?

Yes. Otto describes a note editor, clinician review, signature, autosave, and addenda. Review the whole draft and correct it before signing.

Will it capture every detail correctly?

An AI draft can contain errors or omissions. Check it against the encounter, remove unsupported statements, and add necessary clinical context. We do not make a measured accuracy claim here.

What if a patient doesn’t want to be recorded?

Respect the patient’s choice. Ask the Otto team to demonstrate the manual documentation workflow and confirm your consent and recording options before use.

Are audio recordings used for AI training?

The applicable terms need to be confirmed with Otto. Request written answers about subprocessors, training use, recording retention, deletion, and BAA scope. See our security questions.

Are these notes part of a full EHR?

Otto also describes scheduling, telehealth, patient records, and payments. Explore the mental health EHR to see how they connect.
