A few useful answers

Get to know the details.

Do I need a separate video application?

Otto describes integrated telehealth inside its provider workspace. Confirm supported browsers, devices, and patient join requirements during setup.

Can I use Otto for in-person visits too?

Yes. The documentation and patient-record workflows also apply to in-person sessions. Patient consent is still needed when recording audio.

Are group or couples sessions supported?

Confirm your exact visit type with the team. This preview does not establish support for group, couples, or multi-party telehealth.

Where can I review privacy and recording terms?

Start with security and data questions, then request the applicable written terms from Otto.
