A few useful answers

Get to know the details.

What makes an EHR the best fit for a mental health practice?

Look for a fit with your actual visits: documentation format, clinical review, recurring scheduling, telehealth, billing, and record access. For Otto, evaluate the connected path from session preparation through approved chart updates and cash-pay collection.

Who should take a closer look at Otto?

Independent mental health providers interested in a connected workflow, particularly solo, self-pay practices. Discuss additional requirements with the team.

Where should I start if I am switching?

Begin with the switching guides. Inventory records, confirm the receiving workflow, test a sample, and plan continuity before changing systems.
