A few useful answers

Get to know the details.

Why would I consider Otto if I already use SimplePractice?

Consider Otto if you want to evaluate a connected path from visit preparation through the signed note, approved chart changes, and cash-pay collection. Try the complete workflow and decide whether it addresses the repeated work in your day.

Does SimplePractice also have AI features?

Yes. Its Care Aide suite includes Note Taker, Session Sidekick, Client Summaries, and a Treatment Planner labeled beta. Compare the full workflow and the features available on your plan.

Can Otto replace every feature I use today?

Confirm your required capabilities before switching, especially insurance, custom paperwork, group care, prescribing, and team access. The moving checklist helps organize that evaluation.

Will Otto import my SimplePractice records?

Ask Otto to confirm supported file formats, record categories, migration support, timing, and fees. Test a representative sample before planning a full move.
