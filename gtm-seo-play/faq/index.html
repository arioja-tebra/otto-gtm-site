Questions worth asking

Get a clearer picture of Otto.

Direct answers, with links to the details.

Who is Otto designed for?

Otto’s public site describes an AI-native EHR for mental health providers. Evaluate its current workflows against your practice size, specialty, payment model, and record requirements.

Does Otto offer more than AI note drafting?

The public product page also lists scheduling, telehealth, patient records, and payments. See the mental health EHR overview for the workflow.

Which note formats does Otto describe?

Otto lists SOAP and DAP notes. The clinician reviews, edits, and signs the draft. Read about AI progress notes.

What does the advertised trial include?

The current public offer is 10 signed notes or 30 days, whichever comes first, with no card required. See the pricing page for unresolved billing details.

Does a superbill guarantee reimbursement?

Payment depends on the client’s plan, benefits, deductible, eligibility, and submission requirements. A superbill supplies information for a claim. Read the superbill guide.

Are these free tools part of the EHR?

These are standalone browser tools. This resource site does not establish that the same workflows are integrated into the Otto EHR.

Do the free tools upload or save my entries?

The tools calculate and build documents in the browser without submitting entries to a server or saving them in browser storage. Downloads and printouts contain the entered information. Use fictitious client details in this review version.

Can I import existing records or use insurance claims?

Current support and limits need to be confirmed with Otto. Ask for a demonstration using the record formats, payers, and workflows your practice needs.

Where can I review data handling and BAA terms?

Start with the security review questions and request written, Otto-specific answers. This site does not verify BAA availability or recording-retention terms.
