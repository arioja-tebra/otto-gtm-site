About Otto by Tebra

Otto by Tebra is an AI-native EHR for mental health providers.

The public product brings together AI-drafted notes, clinician review, scheduling, telehealth, patient records, and payments. This resource site helps independent practices work through the business questions around that care.

Practical support between sessions.

The journal covers session rates, superbills, and Good Faith Estimates. Companion tools show their assumptions and let you prepare a document or calculation using your own inputs.

Articles link to their sources and include a date. We avoid unsupported outcome claims and identify where a calculation or workflow needs a practice-specific decision.
