Useful intelligence, inside the work

A draft to start from.
A record you stand behind.

Otto drafts the note from session audio, then gives you room to make it accurate. After you sign, it proposes diagnoses, treatment-plan items, and medication updates for the chart.

  • Choose SOAP or DAP for the draft.
  • Edit and sign the note yourself.
  • Confirm or dismiss each proposed clinical update.
  • Return to the next session with refreshed context.

A few useful answers

Get to know the details.

What makes Otto different?

Otto connects the visit from preparation to documentation, approved chart updates, and cash-pay collection. The practical difference to evaluate is how context and the next action carry forward after each step. Watch the full workflow.

Who is Otto designed for?

Independent mental health providers, with the early experience centered on solo, self-pay practices. Tell us about your specialty, practice size, and billing model so we can confirm fit.

Does AI make clinical decisions for me?

You remain responsible for clinical decisions and the record. Review and edit the draft, sign the final note, and confirm or dismiss proposed structured chart updates.

Does Otto support insurance billing or e-prescribing?

Confirm these requirements with the team before choosing Otto. The workflow described here covers cash-pay collection; it does not promise an insurance-claims or prescribing service.

Can I move from another EHR?

Use the switching guides to prepare your record inventory and questions. Confirm accepted imports, setup support, timing, and fees directly with Otto.

What does Otto cost?

Contact the Otto team for pricing and billing details. Current signup terms govern your account.
