Switching from TherapyNotes

A plan for your TherapyNotes records.

We start with the exports you can obtain, then confirm how the receiving system will handle them.

Before you begin

These are preparation steps for evaluating a move to Otto. Otto’s accepted imports, migration service, timing, and fees still need confirmation. There is no migration upload on this site.

1. Confirm access and responsibility.

Identify who may export the practice’s records, which clients are in scope, who will retain the original records, and how the export will be stored and transferred securely.

2. Understand the source export.

TherapyNotes documents individual and multiple note downloads from Patients → patient name → Documents. The multiple-download flow lets you select documents and produces a combined PDF for that patient.

Use the current TherapyNotes download guide to check permissions and steps. Ask support separately about demographics, insurance details, scheduling, billing, uploaded attachments, and any record categories outside that note download.

Record how many charts and files you expect, then inspect the export before sending anything to a receiving system. Confirm whether a document becomes a readable attachment or structured data in the destination.

3. Match each record category to a destination.

Record categoryAcceptance question for Otto
Demographics and insuranceWhich fields and file formats are supported?
Historical notes and signaturesCan we preserve dates, authorship, signature information, and readability?
Attachments and formsWhat uploads are supported, and how will they attach to the correct client?
Appointments and balancesCan these transfer, or will the practice recreate them?
Audit and access recordsWhat stays in an accessible archive, and who owns retention?

4. Test, reconcile, and plan cutover.

Use an agreed sample to compare counts, dates, labels, and document contents. Keep an exception list and resolve mismatches before approving a broader move. Budget overlap time using the EHR cost calculator.
