A few useful answers

Get to know the details.

Can I schedule recurring sessions?

Otto’s public feature list includes recurring appointments as well as editing and rescheduling.

Can I block time away?

Yes. Otto lists time-off blocks and conflict checking among its scheduling features.

Does this include patient self-booking and reminders?

Confirm the current self-booking, reminder, and communication options for your account. This page does not promise those capabilities.

Can I manage a multi-provider practice?

Tell the team about provider seats, access levels, supervision, and shared scheduling needs so they can confirm current fit.
