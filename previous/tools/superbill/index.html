Out-of-network receipt

Pick the code that matches the care

Common psychotherapy codes: 90832 for 30-minute psychotherapy, 90834 for 45-minute psychotherapy, and 90837 for 60-minute psychotherapy. 90791 is a psychiatric diagnostic evaluation without medical services. Verify the billed code, time, diagnosis, place of service and payer rules against the clinical record before giving this to a client.

A superbill is a receipt for a client to submit to their insurer. It is not a claim or a promise of reimbursement. Include only information the client has agreed to share.
