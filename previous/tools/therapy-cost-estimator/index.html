An estimate starts with the allowed amount.

For each eligible session, the model applies the smaller of the fee and the allowed amount to the remaining deductible. It then applies the plan’s share to the eligible amount left over. Client cost is the session fee minus the estimated plan payment.

Any fee above the allowed amount stays in the client’s estimated cost here. See HealthCare.gov’s definition of out-of-network coinsurance. Confirm coverage, allowed amounts, deductible treatment, and submission requirements with the insurer.
