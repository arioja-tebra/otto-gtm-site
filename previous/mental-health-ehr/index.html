Mental health EHR

Your practice day,
with more room for care.

Otto by Tebra brings AI-drafted notes, patient records, scheduling, telehealth, and payments into one workflow for mental health providers.

Explore the workflow ↗

Follow the visit from prep to payment.

Prepare from the patient record, capture the session with consent, review the note, and confirm the changes that belong in the chart.

Prepare for the session

Review patient summaries and last-session highlights alongside the record. Use the clinical source information to check the context before a visit.

Review your draft

Otto describes audio-based drafts in SOAP or DAP format. Edit the content and sign the final note after your review.

Confirm chart updates

After signing, review proposed diagnoses, plan items, and medication updates. Confirm what should enter the longitudinal record.

Manage the calendar

Use week and agenda views, recurring appointments, time-off blocks, and conflict checking to organize the provider day.

Meet over telehealth

The product site lists waiting rooms, prejoin media checks, and secure join links. Review the service terms and your consent workflow before use.

Close out payment

Review prefilled procedure codes and payment details. Otto lists Stripe Connect, fee schedules, receipts, refunds, and balance alerts.

Check the fit for your practice.

Start with the visits you actually run. Evaluate note formats, scheduling patterns, payment collection, record access, and the work needed to move from your existing system. Ask about any insurance-claims, migration, or multi-provider requirements before committing.
