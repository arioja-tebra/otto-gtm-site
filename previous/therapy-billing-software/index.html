A few useful answers

Get to know the details.

Does a prefilled code guarantee correct billing?

No. You must verify that the code, service, duration, documentation, and fee are appropriate before finalizing the charge.

Does Otto submit insurance claims?

Confirm current insurance support directly with the team before choosing Otto. This page describes cash-pay collection and does not promise claims submission, eligibility, or reimbursement.

Is the free superbill generator part of the EHR?

The free superbill generator is a separate browser tool in this resource site. Its presence does not establish an integrated superbill workflow inside Otto.

What payment fees apply?

Confirm processing fees, subscription terms, and any usage charges before subscribing. The pricing page lists the details to resolve.
