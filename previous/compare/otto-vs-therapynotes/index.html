A few useful answers

Get to know the details.

Does TherapyNotes also offer AI documentation?

Yes. TherapyFuel includes note drafting, Scribe, revision features, treatment-plan generation, and preparation support. Confirm the exact note types and visit eligibility you need.

What should I test in Otto?

Follow a fictional visit through preparation, note review and signature, chart-update confirmation, and payment review. Evaluate the handoffs as well as the draft itself.

Is Otto less expensive?

Total cost depends on your plan, users, optional features, and transactions. Otto’s billing unit and complete fee schedule need confirmation. Use the calculator with verified quotes.

Can I keep access to my records while switching?

Agree on exports, retention, access, and a cutover plan with both vendors. Do not close the old account until the responsible practice owner has accepted the transition.
