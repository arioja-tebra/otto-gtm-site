A few useful answers

Get to know the details.

Does Otto change the chart automatically?

Otto proposes structured clinical updates from the signed note. Those changes require your confirmation before entering the longitudinal record.

Should I rely only on the AI summary?

Use the summary to orient yourself, then check relevant source information in the record. Clinical interpretation and decisions remain yours.

Is a proposed treatment-plan item a complete treatment plan?

A proposed chart item is part of the update workflow. Confirm requirements for standalone treatment-plan documents, signatures, and review schedules separately.

Can my historical records be imported?

Confirm supported record categories, file formats, and migration assistance with Otto. Our switching guides help you prepare the questions.
