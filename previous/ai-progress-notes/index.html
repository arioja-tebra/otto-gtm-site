AI clinical documentation

A draft to review.
Time to think.

With patient consent, Otto uses session audio to draft a structured SOAP or DAP note. You review the content, make corrections, and sign.

Explore note review ↗

Keep clinical judgment in the review.

An AI draft needs a clinician who knows the encounter. Check the details, remove unsupported statements, and make sure the final record reflects your assessment and the care provided.

Capture with consent

Use a consent process appropriate to your setting and applicable requirements. Confirm how recording, retention, and deletion work before starting.

Choose a note format

Otto’s public page lists SOAP and DAP notes. Check the structure and edit the draft to fit your documentation requirements.

Review and sign

Inspect diagnoses, medication details, interventions, risk-related statements, and plans. Sign only after you have checked the draft against the encounter.

What happens after the signature?

Otto describes a separate step for reviewing structured information extracted from the signed note. The clinician confirms or dismisses proposed additions and updates before they enter the patient record.

This review step should remain visible in any demonstration. Ask to see an incorrect suggestion corrected, a proposed update dismissed, and the record after confirmation.

Try a concrete evaluation

Use a fictitious encounter with a clear treatment-plan change. Review the note, correct one detail, then check what Otto proposes for the chart. Confirm that the final record matches your decisions.
