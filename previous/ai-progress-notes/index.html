Two familiar formats

SOAP or DAP.
Your way of documenting.

Use the structure that fits your practice. SOAP separates subjective reports, observations, assessment, and plan. DAP brings the visit information together under data, assessment, and plan.

A few useful answers

Get to know the details.

Can I edit the AI-generated note?

Yes. Otto provides a draft for your review. You can edit it before signing and use the available addendum workflow for later corrections.

Does Otto support custom note templates?

The formats described in this preview are SOAP and DAP. Confirm any need for reusable custom templates, specialty-specific forms, or other formats with the team.

What happens after I sign?

Otto proposes structured updates from the signed note. Review and confirm the items you want in the patient record, then continue through the payment workflow.

How are recording and patient data handled?

Review the security and data questions with the Otto team, including the applicable BAA, recording consent, retention, and AI processing terms.
